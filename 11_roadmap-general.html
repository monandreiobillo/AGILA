i

First-time jobseeker benefit

Under Republic Act No. 11261, eligible first-time jobseekers may receive fee waivers for specified employment documents, including qualifying barangay clearances, NBI clearances, and birth certificates. A barangay certification confirming first-time-jobseeker status is required.

Eligibility, conditions, and any service or delivery charges must be confirmed with the issuing office or official channel. This does not make every registration or service automatically free.

↗ Read RA 11261

Get barangay certification

Request certification confirming your first-time-jobseeker status.

IN PERSON
View details →

What to do

  • Ask your barangay office for its first-time-jobseeker certification procedure.
  • Confirm its local requirements and any form or oath of undertaking it requires.
  • Keep the issued certification for eligible applications.

Source reference

Legal basis: RA 11261, Section 5

The exact local procedure must be confirmed with your relevant barangay.

Apply for NBI clearance

Complete the online application, then attend your scheduled branch appointment.

ONLINE APPLICATION IN PERSON APPOINTMENT
View details →

What to do

  • Register or sign in through the official NBI clearance portal.
  • Complete your applicant information and follow the first-time-jobseeker application instructions.
  • Select a branch and appointment slot when prompted.
  • Confirm the documents to bring, including your barangay certification and any required undertaking.
  • Attend the appointment for the in-person processing steps.
Sample branch: NBI Clearance Center, Ermita, Manila

Official source

NBI: How to Apply for Clearance Online application: NBI Clearance Portal ↗

Appointment availability, required documents, and the application of the fee waiver must be confirmed through official NBI instructions.

Register with SSS

ONLINE

What to do

  • Check whether you already have an SS number.
  • If you do not, follow the official online registration instructions.

  • Confirm which membership or coverage category applies to your employment situation.

Official source
