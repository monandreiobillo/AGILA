Check your first-time-jobseeker eligibility

RA 11261 covers specified employment documents, including qualifying barangay clearances, NBI clearances, and birth certificates. A barangay certification confirming first-time-jobseeker status is required. Waivers are subject to eligibility and legal conditions.

Do not assume SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, delivery, or service charges are automatically waived.
