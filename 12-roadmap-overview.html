Requirements checklist

You may qualify for a fee waiver. Under RA 11261, fees for certain government documents — including NBI clearance, barangay clearance and birth certificate — can be waived for first-time jobseekers.

  • You must present a barangay certification that you are a first-time jobseeker.
  • The benefit can be used only once.
  • JobStart beneficiaries and those covered by similar exemptions don't qualify.

SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG registrations are separate processes and are not specifically listed in the law. Confirm any costs directly with each agency.
